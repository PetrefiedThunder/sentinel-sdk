**11 confirmed findings: 1 Critical, 2 High, 7 Medium, 1 Low.** All remain unfixed. No product behavior changed. Severity is based on the SDK's human-approval safety purpose: a real host running a rejected action is Critical; executing inputs absent from or different from the approval is High.
